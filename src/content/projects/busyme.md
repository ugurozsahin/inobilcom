Busy.me started as an email-centric productivity app: one place where emails, tasks, events and notes stay connected and in sync. The product has since evolved into an AI-powered assistant that handles tasks, notes, events and contacts from a single conversational interface.

Inobil provided architecture and software development support while the product was in its MVP phase, and helped design the backend and infrastructure that serve the AI assistant reliably at MVP scale.
