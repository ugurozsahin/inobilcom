Busy.me is an AI-powered productivity assistant that helps people manage tasks, notes, events and contacts from a single conversational interface.

Inobil provided architecture and software development support while the product was in its MVP phase, and helped design the backend and infrastructure that serve the AI assistant reliably at MVP scale.
