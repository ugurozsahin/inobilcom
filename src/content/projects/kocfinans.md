Koç Finansman is the consumer finance company of Koç Group, one of Türkiye’s largest conglomerates. Inobil worked as tech lead and consultant within its technology department.

Responsibilities included:

- Redesigning and replacing legacy monolith applications with a RESTful microservice architecture
- Integrating Experian PowerCurve decision management products and third-party government APIs
- Proposing technical and process solutions for business problems and spreading distributed-system design practices
- Refactoring, code reviews and troubleshooting alongside the teams
- Consultancy on agile software development, extreme programming and DevOps practices
- Managing private cloud environments (VMware), container platforms (Docker Swarm) and CI/CD pipelines
