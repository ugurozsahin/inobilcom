NPTN (National Property Transaction Network) is a fully digital ecosystem that connects the parties in a UK home-buying journey: lenders, law firms, estate agents and brokers. It streamlines the transaction through authenticated data sharing.

Inobil contributed across the stack: software and product development, architecture support and the DevOps side of the platform.
