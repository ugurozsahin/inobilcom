After Aviva acquired AIG’s UK business, the brand and its digital estate had to be transitioned. Inobil took on a range of technical roles in this transformation, focused mainly on the rebranding and digital modernisation workstreams.

The work also included the engineering needed to close down a number of legacy projects in a controlled way.
