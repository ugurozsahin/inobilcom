bet365 is one of the world’s largest online betting companies. Through BJSS (now part of CGI), Inobil joined the programme that rewrote bet365’s payment systems on a new micro-frontend and microservice architecture.

Inobil’s role was developer consultant within the engineering teams delivering the new payments platform.
